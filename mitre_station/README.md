## Drawer Faces (3/4 plywood, inset)
Assumes 1/16" gap each side for inset fit. Bay interior width: 19 9/16".

Faces are deliberately taller than their boxes. The box floats inside its face opening,
absorbing carcass error and slide slop and keeping every shallow box a single height.

The surplus is distributed so the deep drawer's box sits 1/4" up inside its face, clearing
the cabinet bottom panel by 5/16". That matters: a loaded 1/2" drawer bottom deflects about
1/8" at the centre, so a box set at the 1/16" face reveal would drag on the deck. Every
other gap between boxes then comes out at 7/8". Reveals are cosmetic; box clearances are
not, and the two are sized separately.

## Drawer Boxes (20" deep)
Sides, front, and back: 1/2 plywood. Bottoms are screwed to the bottom edges of the assembled box (no dado) so they can be replaced, and are sized to the box's outside footprint. Shallow bottoms are 5mm plywood (0.197", not 1/4"); the deep bottom drawer gets 1/2 plywood — 1/4" sags close to an inch over this span once the drawer is loaded.

Finished box heights are 4 11/16" shallow (4 1/2 sides + 5mm bottom) and 10 13/16" deep.
